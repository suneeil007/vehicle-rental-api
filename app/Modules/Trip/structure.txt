What is a Trip?

A Trip starts when a customer takes the vehicle and ends when the vehicle is returned.

Real flow
Booking Approved
      ↓
Vehicle Handed Over
      ↓
TRIP STARTS
      ↓
Vehicle In Use
      ↓
Vehicle Returned
      ↓

Why separate CompleteTripRequest?

Because returning a vehicle is a different business action from creating a trip.

Create Trip

customer

vehicle

pickup date

expected return

starting odometer

Complete Trip

actual return date

return odometer

damage

extra charges

This separation keeps the API clean and prevents accidental updates.

Why picked_up cannot be cancelled

Once the vehicle is handed over:

fuel may have been consumed,

odometer has changed,

insurance coverage has started,

the vehicle is no longer available.

At that point, it becomes a return/complete operation, not a cancellation.
